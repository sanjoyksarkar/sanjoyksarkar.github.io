Web-Based MIS & Operational Reporting

Coordinating programme MIS requirements, data quality and dashboard preparations alongside IT and administration.

01 / Context

The challenge

Programme and management teams need consistent information without unnecessary manual work. A useful MIS must reflect operational requirements and be supported by reporting processes and confident users.

02 / Contribution

My role

As IT & Admin Officer on B-PEMS AugroJatra, I coordinated web-based MIS activities with the MIS consultant and programme team. I supported data collection, data quality and reporting requirements alongside everyday IT and administrative responsibilities.

03 / Delivery

The approach

  • Worked with the MIS consultant and programme team to clarify information and reporting requirements.
  • Supported data collection, data-quality checks and preparations for dashboard development.
  • Provided technical support, training and capacity building to six partner NGOs.
  • Managed Microsoft 365 support, MFA and device management, and supported Windows 11 migration and Cyber Essentials compliance.
  • Handled IT procurement, vendor coordination, asset and inventory management, staff travel and ticketing, field transport, office logistics and maintenance.

Hands-on responsibilities

Delivery in practice

My work connected programme information needs with data preparation, partner support and day-to-day office operations.

Programme and partner support

Worked with programme colleagues and six partner NGOs on technical needs and the use of project systems.

  • Technical support and training
  • Capacity building for six partner NGOs

MIS coordination

Worked with the MIS consultant and programme team on data collection, data quality and reporting requirements.

  • Required data gathered
  • Dashboard preparations completed; dashboard not developed

IT and administration

Kept office technology and practical arrangements moving alongside the MIS work.

  • Microsoft 365, MFA, devices and Windows 11 migration
  • Cyber Essentials compliance support
  • Procurement, vendors, assets and inventory
  • Staff travel, ticketing, field transport and office logistics
04 / Operational value

The result

The required data had been gathered and preparations for dashboard development were completed when the project stopped. The dashboard itself was not developed.

05 / Reflection

What this work reinforced

A system is useful when it fits the work people actually do. Requirements, training and ongoing support are as important as implementation.

This case study describes my contribution at a high level. No internal infrastructure details, sensitive datasets or private dashboards are shared.
