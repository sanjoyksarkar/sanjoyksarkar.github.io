Enterprise IT Operations & Infrastructure

01 / Context

The challenge

Headquarters and field programmes depend on connectivity, working devices and accessible enterprise services. Technical incidents can interrupt collaboration and programme operations, especially in demanding field environments.

02 / Contribution

My role

At Save the Children, I supported enterprise ICT infrastructure and users across headquarters and field environments. At Oxfam, I took on independent field IT responsibilities across Cox’s Bazar and Teknaf. At Winrock, that remit expanded into day-to-day IT management, vendor coordination and wider programme operations.

03 / Delivery

The approach

  • Supported LAN/WAN, connectivity, endpoints, enterprise applications and account-related issues.
  • Troubleshot incidents and coordinated with wider organizational IT teams and service providers.
  • Supported equipment lifecycle activities, procurement and vendor coordination.
  • Trained staff and supported secure, reliable use of productivity and collaboration tools.

Hands-on responsibilities

Scale and growing responsibility

Three roles show how enterprise support experience grew into independent field ownership and broader operational coordination.

Save the Children | 2018–2021

ICT operations within an environment spanning approximately 200+ HQ, 500+ field and 100+ Cox’s Bazar users.

  • Enterprise infrastructure and LAN/WAN
  • Endpoints, applications and account support

Oxfam | 2021–2023

Primary field IT responsibility across Cox’s Bazar and Teknaf, coordinating with wider organizational IT teams.

  • Independent incident troubleshooting
  • Connectivity, devices and field continuity

Winrock International | 2023–2025

Managed day-to-day IT services alongside procurement, vendors, collaboration tools and broader organizational responsibilities.

  • Microsoft 365 and office IT operations
  • Vendor coordination and staff training
04 / Operational value

The result

This work supported the continuity of everyday technology services across headquarters and field operations. It established the infrastructure, troubleshooting and user-support foundation I later brought to MIS and digital health systems.

05 / Reflection

What this work reinforced

Good IT operations combine technical diagnosis with clear communication and follow-through. Understanding the field context helps determine what needs attention first.

This case study describes my contribution at a high level. No internal infrastructure details, sensitive datasets or private dashboards are shared.
